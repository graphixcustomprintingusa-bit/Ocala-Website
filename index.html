What we do

Three ways to wear your idea

Every method has its strengths. Here's how to choose, or let the Method Finder below pick for you.

01

Screen Printing

  • Bold, solid colors that last
  • Lower cost per shirt as quantity goes up
  • Great on tees, hoodies and totes

Best for: events, teams, businesses and bulk orders

02

DTF / Digital

  • Unlimited colors and photo detail
  • Great for one-offs and small runs
  • Names and numbers on every piece

Best for: gifts, birthdays, memorial tees and small batches

03

Embroidery

  • Premium stitched, textured look
  • Built to last on workwear
  • Perfect for hats, polos and jackets

Best for: company uniforms, hats and a professional look
